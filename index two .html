Who We Are

A Ministry Built on
Faith, Prayer & Power

The Divine Sisters and Brothers Prayer Ministry International Inc. (DSBPM) is a Spirit-filled Christian ministry headquartered in Benin City, Nigeria. We are known for our powerful intercessory prayer, prophetic services, and digital gospel broadcasts that reach believers around the world.

We are called to empower, equip, and transform lives through the power of the Holy Spirit — in our local community and across the globe through DSBPM TV.

✦ Spiritual Empowerment
Equipping believers to walk in divine authority and purpose.
✦ Prophetic Ministry
Speaking life and direction through the prophetic anointing.
✦ Gospel Broadcast
Taking the Gospel to the world through DSBPM TV.
✦ Community Impact
Transforming lives and communities through the love of Christ.
