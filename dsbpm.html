Who We Are

A Ministry Built on
Prayer & Purpose

The Divine Sisters and Brothers Prayer Ministry International Inc. is a Spirit-filled Christian ministry dedicated to spiritual empowerment, prophetic declaration, and the global spread of the Gospel. Rooted in faith, we serve believers and seekers alike.

✦

Spiritual Empowerment

Equipping the body of Christ through targeted prayer, teaching, and the gifts of the Holy Spirit.

✦

Prophetic Ministry

Operating in the prophetic to bring clarity, direction, and divine breakthroughs to lives.

✦

Digital Gospel Broadcast

Reaching the world through DSBPM TV — live worship, teachings, and prayer sessions streaming globally.

What We Do

Our Ministry Areas

Rooted in the Word, led by the Spirit — our ministry serves across these key areas of spiritual life and growth.

01

Intercessory Prayer

Corporate and individual prayer sessions that stand in the gap for nations, families, and personal needs. We believe in the power of united prayer.

02

Prophetic Services

Spirit-led prophetic declarations, personal ministry, and prophetic worship that bring alignment and divine direction to believers.

03

Gospel Broadcasting

Through DSBPM TV on YouTube, we broadcast worship sessions, teachings, and revival services to a worldwide audience every week.

04

Spiritual Empowerment

Bible-based teachings, discipleship programs, and faith-building conferences designed to equip every believer to walk in spiritual authority.

05

Deliverance Ministry

Ministering freedom and healing to those in bondage, operating in the power of the Holy Spirit to break chains and restore wholeness.

06

Community Outreach

Extending the love of God beyond the walls of the church into the community — feeding, encouraging, and transforming lives.
